
     3. Expressibility & Entanglement Analysis
     A "Fair" comparison isn't just about parameter counts; it's about the Hilbert Space.
      * Scientific Rigor: Add a script that calculates the Meyer-Wallach Entanglement Measure or the Expressibility (using Kullback-Leibler divergence) of your QNN
        circuit.
      * The Argument: If your circuit has high expressibility but low accuracy, it's "overfitting" the Hilbert space. This provides a deep theoretical reason why a
        specific quantum architecture succeeds or fails.
